How battles work

Each Pokemon fights using its own real stats (Attack, Defense, Sp. Attack, Sp. Defense, Speed, and HP), so a well-built team makes a real difference.

Move damage takes into account the move's type versus the target's type (super effective, resisted, or immune, just like the mainline games), plus a same-type bonus when a Pokemon uses a move that matches its own type. There's also a bit of random variance on every hit, so the same move won't always deal the exact same damage.

Opponent trainers lean toward their strongest, most effective move more often, but they don't always pick it, so battles stay a little unpredictable instead of fully random or fully optimal.

Battles play out automatically, both sides just attack each other exchange after exchange. Between exchanges you get a short window (about 1.5 seconds) to use a Potion, Revive, or voluntarily Switch, if you don't act in time, the battle just moves on to the next exchange.

Struggle

If both Pokemon in a battle land a hit that has no effect (pure type immunity, e.g. a Normal move into a Ghost-type) for 6 exchanges in a row, both are forced to use Struggle on the next exchange, a typeless attack that always connects but also deals recoil damage to whoever uses it.

Shiny Pokemon

Every wild encounter, starter pick, and Legendary/Mythical encounter has a small, independent chance of turning up Shiny.

Multi-path evolution

Some Pokemon have more than one possible evolution path (e.g. Eevee, Poliwhirl, Kirlia). In those cases, the game randomly rolls which path your Pokemon takes, with equal odds between every option.

Exception, Mega Evolution: Pokemon with two different Mega Evolutions (Charizard, Mewtwo, Raichu, among others) let you manually choose which one to use at the moment of Mega Evolving.

Fishing

During Cruise Ship stops, a Fishing screen offers 3 free casts (usable only once for the whole run, not per stop), each a shot at a water-dwelling Pokemon. Buy Fishing Bait right there for +1 cast each, as many times as you can afford, Gold is the only limit.

Token Casino (Lucky Dice)

Unlocked at the PokeStop once the endgame opens (8th badge, or reaching the Cruise Ship, whichever comes first). Spend Gold to roll a set of dice for Tokens, one roll at a time or five at once, then spend Tokens in the Token Shop.

Automatic save

The game automatically saves your progress during a run. If you close your browser mid-run, you can pick up where you left off when you come back, the save stays available for up to 7 days. As a Guest this only works on the same device, but if you're signed in it's tied to your account, so you can also resume on a different device.
